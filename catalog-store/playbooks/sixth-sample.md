# Sixth Sample

## What worked
- Official product index `https://sixthsample.com/plugins/` lists the full current line (12 products, bundles excluded as suites).
- Product pages: `https://sixthsample.com/<slug>/` for cramit, deelay, ssf, deelay-pe, utopia (individual URLs for others not confirmed verbatim — use index, do not guess slugs).
- Third-party release coverage verified 2026-09-15: Cramit 1.1.1 (rekkerd.org, conf 90), SSF 2.0 (Cakewalk forum, conf 70), Deelay 1.2.1 (recording.de thread title only, conf 65).
- Update model: free plugins direct download; commercial via on-site cart.

## Caveats
- Cramit vs Cramit PE / Deelay vs Deelay PE are separately listed paid products — distinct identities, do not merge; matcher should prefer longer exact match.
- Official spellings: "F(l)atter", "Spoton", "NASE", "Clips" — copy exactly.
- Aurbit, F(l)atter, NASE, Cramit PE, Clips, Spoton: formats not explicitly observed — verify before use; Utopia + 6 commercial plugins have no public version — leave versionless.
- Several plugins co-credited with Integraudio; Sixth Sample is manufacturer of record.
