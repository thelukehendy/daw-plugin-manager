## Caveats
- Efektor line-wide stamp 1.2.0 explicitly excludes 2026 releases Blues Barker and Blues River — do not apply older line-wide announcements to newer products.
- Versions read from announcements assume announcement date == shipped version; prefer downloads page for confirmation where available.
