# Wave Arts

## What worked
- Public catalog at `https://wavearts.com/products` lists all products (18 observed 2026-09-15).
- Public downloads directory at `https://wavearts.com/downloads` — installers visible without account.
- FAQ at `https://wavearts.com/support/faq` documents update method (direct download).

## Caveats
- No public per-product version numbers on product pages; changelog via downloads page only.
- Suite/component relationships were explicitly not stamped — all 18 products listed as individually installable identities.
