# Tritik

## What worked
- Official FAQ `https://www.tritik.com/faq/` confirms direct-download update model.
- Per-product official changelogs under `https://www.tritik.com/releaseNotes/<product>/changeLog.php` (e.g. KrushPro verified 2026-09-15: v1.1.4 latest, 27 Mar 2023).
- Current version shown on product pages as "version X.Y.Z - changelog" (Moodal 1.2.1, Tymee 1.0.0 verified 2026-09-15).
- KVR Audio news carries Tritik's own update announcements (Krush 1.3.0, Krush2 1.0.1, Visu 1.2.0, TkDelay 1.5.0, Echorus 1.1.0 — all verified 2026-09-15, confidence 90).

## Caveats
- Krush (legacy, still downloadable, unmaintained) vs Krush2 (separate install, successor) — distinct identities; recommend predecessor/successor linkage, do not merge.
- Flaw and Irid: no public current version found 2026-09-15 — leave versionless, do not stamp.
- Bare-word names (Visu, Flaw): matcher needs word-boundary handling.
