Description

The UAD Producer Edition from Universal Audio offers 22 professional instruments and plug-ins full of rich analog tones heard on decades of classic albums, no UA hardware required. Featuring award-winning instruments like Electra 88 Vintage Keyboard Studio and Moog Minimoog to iconic 1176 compression and classic studio ambience effects, the UAD Producer Edition offers everything you need to write, record and mix chart-topping hits. UAD Producer Edition runs natively on Mac or PC, and works with any compatible DAW, including Pro Tools, Logic, Ableton, FL Studio, Cubase, Studio One, Reaper, LUNA and more.

Electra 88 Vintage Keyboard Studio
Channel the sounds from the '70s with the Vintage Keyboard Studio, a fully stocked "vintage" studio of production tools for in-the-box tones nearly indistinguishable from the real thing.

Moog Minimoog
 Add the fat, musically expressive sounds of the most iconic synthesizer ever made with the industry-leading expertise in circuit modeling, synthesis and signal processing.

Opal Morphing Synthesizer
 A flagship synth that's perfect for producers, sound designers and musicians, Opal is an analog-meets-wavetable synth that gives users huge-sounding morphing filters and powerful UA effects.

Ravel
 Perfect for modern music productions of any genre, Ravel offers album-ready sonics and inspiring playability with breathtaking emulation of a Steinway Model B grand piano that goes far beyond other plug-ins and sample libraries with unprecedented realism.

Waterfall B3 Organ
 Explore the world's most thorough emulation of the classic Hammond B3 organ and its companion Leslie 147 rotary speaker cabinet. These give your music the same rich tone and three-dimensional sound heard on decades of legendary albums.

Waterfall Rotary Speaker
 Immerse yourself in the jaw-dropping sounds of the classic Leslie 147 rotary speaker cabinet. Users can envelope guitars, vocals, keyboards and more in chewy, three-dimensional modulation effects for classic textures heard on decades of legendary albums.

1176 Classic Limiter Collection
 Give character and punch to vocals, guitars, bass, drum and anything else with three distinct versions of this easy-to-use limiter, used on more records than any compressor ever made.

Studio D Chorus
 An exacting emulation of a late-'70s Roland Dimension D, the Studio D Chorus adds the same "secret sauce" analog widening effects for stunning analog modulation textures. 

Brigade Chorus
 Flatter your guitars, vocals and synths with the luxuriant analog modulation effects of a vintage BOSS CE-1 Chorus Ensemble and enjoy three-dimensional movement and shimmer.

PolyMAX Synth
 This vintage-inspired synth gives users fat analog tones from the golden age of polysynthesis wrapped up with warm, analog-modeled oscillators and filters. It's full of professional UA effects and curated presets that easily fit into any genre for instant, album-ready sound.

Century Tube Channel Strip
 Enhance your production with a vintage-style tube mic preamp, simple three-band EQ and optical compressor designed to give you everything you need to capture the magic.

Pultec Passive EQ Collection
 Featuring three Pultec EQs that are indistinguishable from the original analog hardware, get the same silky curves heard on hit records for your production.

Teletronix LA-2A Classic Leveler Collection
 Get smooth sounds with three highly sought-after versions of the most famous optical tube compressor ever made.

Galaxy Tape Echo
 Add subtle tape delay and colorful cacophony to your production with the definitive emulation of an iconic analog contraption.

Pure Plate Reverb
 Experience UAD's revolutionary plate reverb emulation in a simple, easy-to-use package. Boasting all of the essential features, it'll give your vocals, guitars, strings and synths lush a satisfying ambience.

Oxide Tape Recorder
 Based on UAD's award-winning Ampex and Studer tape machine emulations, Oxide Tape Recorder gives users the warmth, presence and vibe of professional analog tape. It features only the essential controls, so you can get sounds faster and make everything sound crisp and clean.
