The Softube Flow Mixing Suite is a powerful tool designed to streamline your audio mixing workflow. It combines over 20 years of professional audio development with a unique plug-in subscription model, allowing users to access a growing collection of high-quality plug-ins. The suite aims to provide pro audio results quickly by utilizing signal chains created by experienced music producers and macros for fast adjustments and creative exploration.

With the Flow Mixing Suite, you gain access to a library of over 50 plug-ins, including essential tools like EQs, compressors, and reverbs, as well as creative options like guitar amps. These plug-ins can be used seamlessly within the Flow environment or individually as separate plug-ins in your preferred Digital Audio Workstation (DAW). The subscription model offers a distinct advantage, allowing users to subscribe monthly and perpetually own their tools, a concept Softube describes as "better than a subscription."

Key features of the Flow Mixing Suite are designed to enhance your mixing process. Flow Controls provide a fast workflow through intuitively designed macro control over signal chains. You can also create custom mixing chains with User Signal Chains, including the option to make simplified one-to-one macros. Additionally, Flow Packs, continually released and created by professional producers, offer inspiring and creative options to explore different production techniques. The suite is designed to help you achieve a flow state by going beyond typical EQ/compression mix rack plug-ins, incorporating reverbs, delays, and pitch correction for a more comprehensive mixing experience.

Features:

  • Flow Controls: Fast workflow with intuitively designed macro control of signal chains.
  • User Signal Chains: Create custom mixing chains with simplified one-to-one macros.
  • Flow Packs: Access continually released, producer-created Flow Packs.
  • Access to over 50 plug-ins (EQs, compressors, reverbs, guitar amps, etc.).
  • Plug-ins usable within Flow or as separate DAW plug-ins.
  • Subscription model for perpetual plug-in ownership.
  • Professionally built signal chains with macros, contextual information, and visualizations.
  • Designed to incorporate reverbs, delays, and pitch correction.
  • Consultation with "Flow Collective" of modern music producers for built-in Flows.
  • Downloadable Flow Packs for extra creative versatility.
  • Benefits for Console 1 owners, including access to Core Mixing Suite and Console 1-ready plug-ins.
