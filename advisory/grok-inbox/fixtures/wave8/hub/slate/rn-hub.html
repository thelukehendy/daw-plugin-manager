Slate Digital Connect 1.4.0

August 25, 2022

What's new?

  • You can now sign in on Slate Digital Connect with your Slate Digital account! When signed in, you can access the new My Products page in the menu. There, you will be able to see every plugin you purchased, and if you have the All Access Pass, you will also see all the products you have access to! No more searching around in the whole list to find that one plugin you just bought. But not to worry—you can still use Slate Digital Connect without signing in.

  • ANA 2 Expansion Packs are now downloadable directly from Slate Digital Connect if you have the All Access Pass.
  • A lot of under-the-hood changes have been made to the UI. We are working hard on making the interface consistent, but this means we had to (and still have to) make a lot of changes to the core of the application. Please be patient!
  • The window now has a minimum size. No more shrinking us down to a 1 pixel window in the corner of the screen.
  • Some minor fixes have been implemented to squash some of those nasty bugs.

  • See available plugins updates and update all in one click

  • Choose your VST2 path

  • Have info on Plugins download size
  • Auto update improved (no need to close and reopen your app)
  • Customized experience by login to your slate digital account

WHAT DOES “BETA” MEAN?

With Slate Digital Connect, you can install, manage and update your plugins quickly and easily.

This a beta version. It’s been tested & proven reliable on a wide range of platforms, and we agreed the time has come to share it with all our customers while we keep adding new features. We want to know what you think! Please send us your feedback directly from inside Slate Digital Connect (using the little talk bubble on the bottom right corner). We read every single comment & suggestion and your feedback will guide us as we continue to make Slate Digital Connect your one-stop shop for all your Slate Digital products.
