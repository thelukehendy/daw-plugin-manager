Pigments Play
Next-gen sounds for producers

Create forward

Up the innovativeness and impact of your production with Pigments Play, a free software synthesizer powered by Pigments. Explore 100 expertly curated presets, then reshape them through a focused, audio-reactive interface with six forms of synthesis, 50+ filter modes and 20 effects.

Get a download link sent to your email address to install Pigments Play on your computer:

Modern synthesis
made immediate

Experience the power of Pigments in an approachable instrument for first-time sound designers and fast-moving producers. Start from a polished preset, see it respond as you play, and make meaningful changes without losing your flow.

Find your sound
fast

From luminous keys and liquid pads to heavyweight basses, animated sequences and razor-edged leads, discover 100 sounds chosen for modern pop, R&B, garage, house, drum and bass, trap, ambient and beyond.

Pigments power
distilled

Move through Modal, Granular, Wavetable, Sample, Harmonic and Virtual Analog synthesis, then explore 50+ filter modes and 20 effects. A focused set of controls keeps every change clear, immediate and musical.

Play your sounds
with any controller

Whether you own a MiniLab, a KeyLab, a KeyStep or a third-party controller, we've got you covered.

The MIDI Integration, implemented in all Pigments versions to get playing right away with your controller of choice.

Main Features

100 Presets created by expert sound designers from Pigments

One simple interface with 4 macros: brightness, timbre, time, movement and 4 effects: 2 mod effects, delay, reverb

Updated browser to find sounds quickly: filter by type and instrument

Sound Store: 200+ Artist & Genre based sound banks

New paid and Free content added regularly

Automatic mapping of parameters for Arturia keyboards

Parameters can be mapped easily to other MIDI controllers

Platform Specifications

Windows

  • Win 10+ (64bit)
  • 4 GB RAM
  • 4 cores CPU, 3.4 GHz (4.0 GHz Turbo-boost)
  • 4GB free hard disk space
  • OpenGL 2.0 compatible GPU
  • ARM processors not supported on Windows

Required configuration

  • Works in Standalone, VST, AAX, Audio Unit.

Apple

  • Mac OS 11+
  • 4 GB RAM
  • 4 cores CPU, 3.4 GHz (4.0 GHz Turbo-boost) or M1 CPU
  • 4GB free hard disk space
  • OpenGL 2.0 compatible GPU
